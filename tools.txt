pandoc for converting tex to markdown:

- command: pandoc -f markdown -t latex filename.tex -o filename.md


Tikz magic (not currently used):

Convert PDF to PNG:
- pdftoppm -png input.pdf output  (don't add extension -- auto-generated)
- convert input.pdf -quality 300 -colorspace RGB output.png  (blurry result)
